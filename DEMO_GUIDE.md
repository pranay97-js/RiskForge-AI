# Razorpay RiskForge AI — Product Walkthrough & Operations Manual

> **Product Scope:** Real-Time Fraud & Chargeback Detection, Tri-Signal Verification, Explainable Decisioning, and Grounded Dispute Response  
> **Workflow:** Predict $\longrightarrow$ Verify $\longrightarrow$ Explain $\longrightarrow$ Act

---

## 🧭 Platform Architecture & Operational Workspaces

### 1. Executive Command Center (`🌐 Executive Command Center`)
- **Real-Time Portfolio Exposure:** Aggregated volume, total monetary exposure at risk, and active chargeback rates across processed streams.
- **3D Risk Threat Visualizer:** Interactive WebGL particle mesh reflecting live risk states:
  - 🟢 **Settlement Cleared (`LOW`):** Normal baseline velocity and non-anomalous purchasing patterns.
  - 🟡 **Anomaly Review (`REVIEW`):** Ambiguous signals requiring operational inspection or step-up authentication.
  - 🔴 **Elevated Threat (`HIGH`):** Extreme deviations, high velocity, or prior dispute records.
- **Search & Filter Operations:** Filter by Transaction Reference ID, Customer Entity, Payment Channel (UPI, Card, Netbanking, Wallet), or 3DS authentication status.

---

### 2. Transaction Investigator (`🔍 Transaction Investigator`)
- **Tri-Signal Diagnostic Suite:**
  1. **Calibrated ML Risk Score:** Continuous chargeback probability generated by the trained XGBoost model.
  2. **Deterministic Security Controls:** 6 rule checks (`VELOCITY_ANOMALY`, `SPENDING_DEVIATION`, `DISPUTE_HISTORY`, `NEW_ACCOUNT_HIGH_VALUE`, `PAYMENT_CHANGE`, `DATA_INSUFFICIENT`).
  3. **TreeSHAP Attributions:** High-precision feature contributions demonstrating which factors increased risk (e.g., velocity burst, spending deviation) and which factors provided protection.
- **Financial Exposure Breakdown:** Direct calculation of $P(\text{chargeback}) \times (\text{Amount} + \text{Network Penalty})$ and expected recovery savings.

---

### 3. Live Simulator & Human Review (`✍️ Live Simulator & Human Review`)
- **Manual Transaction Input:**
  - Enter custom transaction amounts, customer identifiers, payment methods, card networks, client device types, and foreign IP countries.
  - Toggle 3D-Secure authentication, cold-start new account flags, and dispute histories.
  - Pre-load standard operational profiles (Clean UPI, High-Value Luxury, Velocity Spike, Cross-Border Non-3DS).
- **Real-Time Risk Scoring:** Instant evaluation through the end-to-end feature extraction, ML, rules, cost, and fusion pipeline.
- **Human-in-the-Loop Decisioning & Audit Ledger:**
  - Analyst Actions: **Approve**, **Step-Up 2FA/KYC**, **Block & Refund**, or **Forward to Dispute Defense**.
  - Capture investigation notes and commit them to the live session audit ledger for full compliance and auditing.

---

### 4. Dispute Defense & Evidence Center (`📑 Dispute Defense & Evidence Center`)
- **Compelling Evidence 3.0 Checklist:**
  - 1. Gateway Settlement Receipt & Payment ID
  - 2. 3D-Secure Authentication Logs
  - 3. Signed Courier Proof of Delivery (POD) / Waybill
  - 4. Customer Order Confirmation & Chat Logs
- **Dynamic Completeness Meter:** Scores evidence readiness from 0% to 100%.
- **Zero-Hallucination Grounded AI Responder:**
  - Generates formal, bank-compliant dispute rebuttals strictly from verified transaction parameters and present documentation.
  - Refuses contestation if critical proof (e.g., delivery signature) is missing, alerting merchants to retrieve necessary documents.

---

### 5. Risk Engine Intelligence (`📊 Risk Engine Intelligence`)
- **Empirical Held-Out Performance:**
  - PR-AUC: `0.7331` (vs `0.4279` baseline).
  - ROC-AUC: `0.9327`.
  - False-Positive Reduction: `59.1%` reduction in false alarms.
- **Bayesian Threshold Optimization:** Interactive frontier curve balancing manual investigation cost against dispute penalties to identify cost-optimal decision cutoffs.
